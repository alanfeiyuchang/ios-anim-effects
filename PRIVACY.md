# Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

_Motionary – Motion Dictionary · Last updated: September 25, 2026 · 最后更新：2026 年 9 月 25 日_

## English

Motionary does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** The app contains no third-party SDKs.
- **No network access.** Every effect, prompt and parameter is built into the app and runs on your device.
- **On-device settings only.** Your language, appearance and favorites are saved locally on your device
  (in the app's own storage) and never leave it. Deleting the app removes them.
- **Clipboard.** When you tap "Copy prompt", the prompt text is placed on your clipboard; the app never reads
  your clipboard.
- **Children.** The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/alanfeiyuchang/motionary-ios-animations/issues
or email alanfeiyuchang@gmail.com.

## 中文

Motionary 不收集、不存储、也不分享任何个人数据。

- **无账号、无追踪、无统计分析、无广告。** App 不包含任何第三方 SDK。
- **不联网。** 所有动效、提示词和参数都内置在 App 里，在你的设备上运行。
- **设置只保存在本机。** 语言、外观和收藏只保存在设备本地（App 自己的存储空间），不会离开设备；删除 App 即一并删除。
- **剪贴板。** 点击「复制提示词」时，提示词会放进你的剪贴板；App 从不读取你的剪贴板。
- **儿童。** App 适合所有年龄，不向任何人（包括儿童）收集信息。

如本政策有变更，新版本会发布在此地址并更新日期。

问题反馈：在 https://github.com/alanfeiyuchang/motionary-ios-animations/issues 提 issue，
或发邮件至 alanfeiyuchang@gmail.com。
